## 🧪 Quick Test Voice Commands
- **Observational Dictation:** `"Record note: Observed clear lysis after 15 min at 56°C"`
- **Reagent Registration:** `"Add reagent 10X PBS Buffer, 500 mL, location Shelf B"`
- **Inventory Adjustment:** `"Deduct 50 mL of Ethanol"`
- **Dilution Calculation:** `"Calculate dilution for 100 mM to 10 mM in 50 mL"`
- **Task Management:** `"Create task: Autoclave pipette tips by Friday"`
- **Modal Control:** `"Open barcode scanner"` / `"Close the barcode scanner"`
- **Capabilities Query:** `"What are the features you have"`
